Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Hold (“we”, “the app”) is an Android screen-time and app-blocker app published as Hold: Screen Time & App Blocker (package app.hold.focus). This policy explains what the app uses on your device and what we do not collect.

Information stored on your device

Hold does not create an account and does not run its own backend. The following stays on your phone unless you clear app storage or uninstall:

  • Optional first name — if you type one during setup, it is stored locally so the home screen can greet you.
  • Apps you chose to pause — package names such as Instagram or TikTok.
  • Today’s pause counts — how many times a selected app came to the front, and whether you continued or went Home. These reset each calendar day.
  • Pro status — whether this install has unlocked Pro, and which plan, after a Google Play purchase (when billing is connected).

Permissions and why they exist

  • Usage access — Hold reads which app is in the foreground so it can show a pause when you open an app you selected. It uses the app’s package name. It does not read message contents, photos, passwords, or your screen.
  • Display over other apps — Hold draws the pause screen on top of the selected app. You can always choose Go Home.
  • Notifications — Android requires a persistent notification while Hold is watching, so the pause can appear after you leave Hold.
  • Internet — used by the operating system and, when you buy Pro, by Google Play Billing. Hold does not send your name, selected apps, or pause history to our own servers.

Information we do not collect

  • No account, email sign-in, or cloud backup of your Hold settings.
  • No advertising identifier and no third-party ads in the current version.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Third-party services

  • Google Play — if you install, update, or buy Pro from the Play Store, Google processes that purchase and related account data under Google’s privacy policy.

Children

Hold is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn Hold off from the home screen to stop watching apps.
  • Revoke Usage access or Display over other apps in Android Settings.
  • Clear Hold’s storage or uninstall the app to delete local data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
